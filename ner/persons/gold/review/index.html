What you are checking

The Tropical Agriculturist (Colombo, 1881–1945) was a journal of plantation agriculture in Ceylon. A computer program found every person named in it and grouped the mentions into people. It then tried to match each person to Wikidata, the open database behind Wikipedia. Your answers tell us how often the program is right.

  1. Read the evidence. The names as printed, the roles the journal gives them, the years and places.
  2. One person or several? Do the roles and years describe one life? Two different careers under one name (e.g. a Ceylon planter and a London chemist) mean mixed.
  3. Wikidata. Open the proposed item and the other candidates. Accept one only if its description, occupation or positions and its dates fit the evidence; the same name is not enough. If none fits, search Wikidata yourself (the search Wikidata link) using the name plus a role. If you find the person, paste the Q-number (e.g. Q2462643). Many planters and Ceylon officials are not in Wikidata, so "not in Wikidata" is a normal answer.
  4. Each mention. Is the highlighted name in the snippet this person? Open the scan if the snippet isn't enough. Mentions linked by "surname only" are the ones most likely to be wrong, so look closely.
  5. Answer what you can; use can't tell rather than guessing. Notes are welcome.
