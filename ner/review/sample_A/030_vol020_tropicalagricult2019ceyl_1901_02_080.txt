#### COFFEE PLANTING IN SUMATRA.

We are always pleased to hear from our old friend "W. T. McK." who, after leaving troops of friends behind in Ceylon, has been so many years now in Sumatra without making a fortune. He sends us as usual a graphic letter (given in our daily and *Tropical Agriculturist*) with some useful statistics, showing that his district of "Serdang" has about 10,000 acres under Liberian coffee. The photograph he sends, shows magnificent giant coffee trees (backed by Albizzias) laden with crop; for, of course, the soil is superior to anything we have in Ceylon. But then the market price of "coffee" has, for a long time, been even more disappointing than that of tea!
