## THE COCONUT PAVILION.

---

The Pavilion made of coconut wood erected by Mudaliyar A. E. Rajapaksa of Negombo at the All-Ceylon Exhibition, which with other exhibits won the cup for the best exhibit of the products of the Coconut palm, has been presented to the Royal Botanic Gardens, Peradeniya, and has been re-erected near the ferry where it commands a fine view of the river and will afford shelter to those waiting to cross.
